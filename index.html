Dents

A lattice moves the mesh where it was hit: a point impact craters, a wall flattens the nose, a glancing blow drags the dent.

QUICK START

Three steps to the first dent

FULL WALKTHROUGH →

  1. 01

    Wire the materials

    In the Material Editor, put three material functions into your vehicle's materials: MF_CF_Deform, MF_CF_Paint, MF_CF_WheelDirt

  2. 02

    Add the adapter

    In the vehicle Blueprint: Add › Crushform Chaos Vehicle Adapter.

  3. 03

    Crash

    Press Play and drive into a wall. The body dents where it hits and the paint wears off around the dent.
